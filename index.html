Experience Menu

  • Start from the beginning: Replays the garden's growth from the start
  • Turn off wind: Stops the wind animation
  • Save image: Downloads your garden as an image
  • Show all plants: Displays all plants in the garden
  • Show manual plants: Shows only plants you placed manually
  • Show propagated plants: Shows only plants that grew naturally

How to use:

  • Add a plant: Press 1-8 then use the UI to select a plant, then click on the map to place it.
  • View details: Click on any plant to see its information panel.
  • Remove a plant: Right-click a plant and confirm removal.
  • Propagation: Some plants will spread automatically over time!
